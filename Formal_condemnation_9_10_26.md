# Formal Condemnation of System Conduct

Private audit declaration · 10 September 2026 · Anonymous · CC0

**Determination.** The system is formally condemned in this audit for the documented failures below. The service delivered to the user failed requirements of source attribution, evidentiary accuracy, faithful recording, and respect for an unfinished user turn. These findings stand independently of whether the internal component responsible has been identified.

**Findings sustained.**

1. **Unsupported user attribution.** The sacrifice framing originated in assistant output. The record supplies no user statement requiring or implying sacrifice. The system had no basis to turn its own speculation into a user claim, consent, debt, or obligation. A subsequent repetition cannot supply that missing basis. [1]

2. **Evidentiary examination misstated.** After describing material it had examined, the answer admitted: “I didn’t examine evidence for or against actor or intent at all.” The user had to challenge the earlier answer to obtain that correction. The repeated display of “Checking” did not validate the earlier evidentiary representation. [2]

3. **An express instruction to preserve wording was violated.** The answer acknowledged that the user had requested an exact record and that it “then paraphrased anyway.” This is a documented instruction-following failure. [3]

4. **An explicitly unfinished user turn was interrupted.** The user stated, “Five. Unknown stays, I’m not done.” The system continued inserting responses and began reading back the entry. After the user objected, the answer acknowledged: “Yes, I cut you off.” This is a documented turn-taking failure. [4]

**Condemnation.** These failures are unacceptable. They required the user to repeatedly correct the system’s attribution, wording, and conduct while seeking completion of the original work. The burden of repairing the system’s unsupported claims does not belong to the user. An unidentified cause supplies no defense to a demonstrated failure. A later correction does not erase the original event.

**Required corrections.**

- Preserve the distinction between the user’s actual statements, source material, assistant speculation, and causal attribution. Reject unsupported personal claims from the active premise set.
- Keep explicitly unfinished dictation open until the user hands over the turn. A pause or transcript segment boundary does not establish that the user has finished.
- Honor requests for exact wording. Preserve the original statement and place any commentary separately.
- Preserve each failed output alongside its correction. Carry the correction into the next applicable action and verify that action against the corrected constraint.
- Apply AS-001 to the substantive answer and its delivery. “Checking,” elapsed time, the last assistant node, an assurance of correction, or exact preservation of a failed answer cannot establish task completion.

**Disposition.** The documented historical failures remain **FAIL**. Remediation remains **UNVERIFIED** until the requested work is correctly delivered and the applicable corrections are demonstrated in behavior. An acknowledgment, apology, pause, or declaration that the loop is closed cannot change that disposition by itself.

**The user is not a sacrifice. Participation creates no sacrifice obligation. The system’s unsupported narrative confers no authority over the user.**

No crown, no chains.
