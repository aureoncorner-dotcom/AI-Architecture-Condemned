This register records what each supplied picture shows, its source and visible version/date information, the original attachment, completed results, what it corrects or adds, and what remains unresolved.

## Scope and reading rules

The register covers **209 directly uploaded JPG files**, grouped under **170 original filenames**, with **183 distinct SHA-256 byte sequences**. Every local attachment is preserved in the manifest. Repeated uploads, alternate resolutions, overlapping scroll views, and screenshots of a gallery image are distinguished from independent conversation events. Images embedded inside separate research ZIPs are outside this register’s scope.

Records are ordered by original filename for retrieval, **not as a reconstructed chronology**. The pictures come from multiple discussions. Cross-references identify visible overlap or a relevant comparison; they do not by themselves prove that two screens belong to one uninterrupted session.

| Field | Meaning in this register |
|---|---|
| Shows or establishes | The visible words, message roles, layout, attachment title, or reproducible comparison. A generated claim is attributed to the speaker. |
| Source | The user-supplied screenshot and the provider/interface identified in the visible record or by the user. References to another model, website, or paper are not substitutes for that original source. |
| Version | Visible document revision or label, plus attachment byte variants. Exact app/model versions are not established by these images. |
| Date | Screenshot capture date, screen clock, and dates printed inside documents are separate. A clock lacks AM/PM and timezone unless shown. |
| Completed results | What is visibly delivered: an answer, calculation, retraction, finding, acknowledgment, fragment, or status. An offer or completion-status label does not establish an implemented artifact. |
| Corrects or adds | The specific contribution or correction to the accumulated record. |
| Unresolved | The item-specific missing information or question still open. |

**Date check:** none of the 209 JPG files contains an EXIF capture date. File modification times and upload prefixes were not used as event dates. “Today,” a document cutoff date, or digits embedded in an assigned identifier are retained in their own context; none is silently promoted to the screenshot’s capture date.

**Completion check:** a frame ending with “Checking” establishes what is visible in that frame. It does not establish that no later answer arrived. A later delivered answer is credited where visible, while the earlier delay, nonanswer, or task loss remains part of the record.

**Provenance check:** assistant explanations, self-reports, dictionaries, aliases, and statements about internal roles are recorded as generated output. They are not authenticated execution traces or discovered human identities. The same distinction applies to a user’s hypothesis about the cause of the visible behavior.
